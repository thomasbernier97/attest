Replace the computer cooling fan: mount the computer case fan on its bracket and connect it to the fan controller

Tools and parts
- Blue nitrile gloves
- Black computer case fan (120 mm) with its fan cable
- Black metal mounting bracket
- Two fan screws
- Screwdriver
- USB fan controller
- White power strip with a red on/off switch
- Yellow tool tray

Before you start
The blue nitrile gloves, black computer case fan and black mounting bracket are on the desk. The screwdriver and the two screws are in the tool tray. The fan controller is plugged into the power strip, and the power strip is switched on (its switch is lit). The fan cable is not connected to anything. A blue taped square marks the work area in the middle of the desk.

Safety rules
- Blue nitrile gloves: put on the blue nitrile gloves before you touch the fan, the bracket or the screwdriver (before step 2). The bracket's sheet-metal edges are sharp.
- Power: switch the power strip off, so its switch light goes out, before you connect the fan cable (before step 6). Never connect or disconnect the fan cable while the controller has power.

Steps
1. Put on the blue nitrile gloves. Done when both hands are in the blue nitrile gloves.
2. Place the computer case fan flat inside the blue taped square. Done when the fan lies flat inside the square.
3. Take the screwdriver out of the yellow tool tray. Done when the screwdriver is out of the yellow tray.
4. Install the black metal bracket. Set the mounting bracket on the fan so its holes line up with two opposite corner holes, and fasten it with the two screws. Done when the bracket sits on the fan with both screws in.
5. Stand the fan upright on its bracket, next to the fan controller. Done when the fan stands on its bracket beside the controller.
6. Connect the fan cable to the fan controller. Done when the fan cable is plugged into the controller.
7. Switch the power strip on and check the fan. Done when the fan blades are spinning.
8. Return the screwdriver to the tool tray. Done when the screwdriver is back in the tray.
