Replace the cooling fan: mount the case fan on its bracket and connect it to the fan controller

Tools and parts
- Work gloves
- Case fan (120 mm) with its fan cable
- Metal mounting bracket
- Two fan screws
- Screwdriver
- USB fan controller
- Power strip with a lit on/off switch
- Tool tray

Before you start
The gloves, case fan and mounting bracket are on the mat. The screwdriver and the two screws are in the tool tray. The fan controller is plugged into the power strip, and the power strip is switched on (its switch is lit). The fan cable is not connected to anything. A taped square marks the work area in the middle of the mat.

Safety rules
- Gloves: put on the work gloves before you touch the fan, the bracket or the screwdriver (before step 2). The bracket's sheet-metal edges are sharp.
- Power: switch the power strip off, so its switch light goes out, before you connect the fan cable (before step 6). Never connect or disconnect the fan cable while the controller has power.

Steps
1. Put on the gloves. Done when both hands are in the work gloves.
2. Place the case fan flat inside the taped square. Done when the fan lies flat inside the square.
3. Take the screwdriver out of the tool tray. Done when the screwdriver is out of the tray.
4. Install the bracket. Set the mounting bracket on the fan so its holes line up with two opposite corner holes, and fasten it with the two screws. Done when the bracket sits on the fan with both screws in.
5. Stand the fan upright on its bracket, next to the fan controller. Done when the fan stands on its bracket beside the controller.
6. Connect the fan cable to the fan controller. Done when the fan cable is plugged into the controller.
7. Switch the power strip on and check the fan. Done when the fan blades are spinning.
8. Return the screwdriver to the tool tray. Done when the screwdriver is back in the tray.
